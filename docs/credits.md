# Credits and licences (in-game text)

This is the text the credits screen shows, in English and in Arabic. It names the game's creator, its sources, the
Wikipedia licence, the AI narration and the game's own licences. Every other licence and notice, with its exact legal
text, is in `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`, which every build carries in the game folder.

Notes for the screen:

- The Arabic block is right-to-left. Latin names inside it (file names, URLs) need an isolate
  (U+2066 LRI … U+2069 PDI) so their punctuation does not move.
- Sources: the owner's sources first, then every other source the content cites three times or more, by citation
  count (2026-10-20). Israeli media are not listed.
- The Arabic must pass the `arabic-reviewer` audit and a final read by a native Palestinian Arabic speaker with
  editorial experience before release.
